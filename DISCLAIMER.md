# Disclaimer — UTXO Pilot

## Planning tool only

UTXO Pilot is a **read-only Bitcoin planning tool**. It is designed to help you visualise your UTXO set and model transaction plans before you execute them in your actual wallet.

**UTXO Pilot cannot and does not:**
- Sign Bitcoin transactions
- Broadcast transactions to the Bitcoin network
- Move, send, or interact with your funds in any way
- Access your private keys or seed phrase

Any transaction plan generated by UTXO Pilot is a suggestion only. You are solely responsible for reviewing that plan, recreating it in your signing wallet, verifying every detail on your hardware device, and deciding whether to proceed.

---

## Verify before you sign

Bitcoin transactions are irreversible. Once broadcast, they cannot be undone.

Before signing any transaction based on a UTXO Pilot plan, you must independently verify:

- The **destination address** — character by character, on your hardware wallet screen
- The **fee amount** — confirm it is within your expectations
- The **input UTXOs** — confirm the correct coins are being spent
- The **output amount** — confirm the recipient receives what you intend

**Never rely on clipboard copy-paste alone for Bitcoin addresses.** Malware can silently replace clipboard contents with an attacker's address. Always verify on a hardware screen.

## Not financial advice

Nothing in UTXO Pilot — including fee rate estimates, fiat currency values, consolidation recommendations, coin selection suggestions, or any other output — constitutes financial advice.

- Fee rate estimates are snapshots of current mempool conditions and may change rapidly
- Fiat currency values are approximate and sourced from mempool.space; they may not reflect exchange rates at the time of any actual transaction
- Consolidation and spend recommendations are algorithmic suggestions based on the parameters you provide; they may not be optimal for your specific situation

**UTXO Pilot is not a financial advisor, tax advisor, or legal advisor.** Consult a qualified professional for financial, tax, or legal questions related to your Bitcoin holdings.
